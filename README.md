## About Me

I am a graduate researcher at [Kyung Hee University](https://www.khu.ac.kr), specializing in **Large Language Models (LLMs)**, **Multimodal AI**, and **Medical AI**. My research focuses on designing agentic AI systems and applying them to healthcare and user experience domains. I have authored and co-authored papers published in IEEE conferences and high-impact journals such as *Psychological Medicine* and *Journal of Medical Systems*.
